## Full description (max 4000)
Build a CV that gets noticed.

DexaCV is an AI-assisted CV and resume builder. Start from a professional template, fill in your details with a live preview beside you, and export a clean, recruiter-ready PDF or an editable Word (DOCX) file — with no watermark.

WHY DexaCV
• 40+ original templates — modern, minimal, creative, executive, elegant, classic and ATS-friendly designs.
• Live preview — see every change land on the page as you type.
• AI writing assistance — turn rough notes into a sharp professional summary and result-focused bullet points.
• Real-text PDF export — correct page breaks, selectable text, no watermark.
• DOCX export — an editable Word document with proper headings and bullet points.
• Works everywhere — a responsive editor built for phone, tablet and desktop.

EVERY SECTION YOU NEED
Personal info, professional summary, work experience, education, skills, languages, projects, certifications, awards, courses, volunteering, interests and references. Reorder, show or hide any section, and restyle your CV at any time without losing a word of your content.

LEARN AS YOU BUILD
• CV examples for developers, designers, students, managers, marketers and accountants — open one and make it yours in minutes.
• Practical guides: how to write a CV, writing one with no experience, professional summaries, ATS-friendly CVs, and role-specific advice.
• Mock Interview Coach — practise realistic interview questions one at a time and get instant feedback tailored to your target role.

START WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
No signup is needed to start building. Your work is auto-saved on your device, and you can create an account whenever you want to keep your projects together.

AVAILABLE IN 4 LANGUAGES
English, Arabic (full right-to-left support), French and Spanish.

PRICING
Building, customizing and previewing your CV is free. Exporting a finished CV costs €0.99 per download — a one-time payment, no subscription.
